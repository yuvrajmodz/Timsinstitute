About TIMS Institute

Built on a belief that quality digital education should be accessible to every aspiring professional in Rajasthan.

Our Mission

Empowering Careers Through Real Skills

TIMS Institute was founded with a clear purpose — to bridge the gap between classroom education and real-world industry requirements. We believe that every student in Sardarshahar deserves access to the same quality of professional training available in big cities.

Our courses are entirely practical. From day one, students work with real tools, real campaigns, and real projects — because we believe that's the only way to build genuine confidence and capability.

Visionary Leadership

Mr. Ram Soni founded TIMS Institute with a vision to bring quality digital education to Sardarshahar and empower local youth with practical skills that lead to real career opportunities.

With years of industry experience and a deep understanding of the digital landscape, he has built an institute that focuses on hands-on learning, industry-relevant curriculum, and genuine student success.
