Internship Rules

Internship & Certification

Our internship is a structured program with clear requirements. Here's exactly what you need to know.

01

Mandatory Internship

An internship component is a compulsory part of the programme for eligible students upon completing their coursework.

02

85% Attendance Required

A minimum of 85% attendance throughout the course duration is required to be eligible for the internship and final certificate.

03

Compulsory Tests

All in-class and module-end tests are compulsory. Missing a test without prior approval will affect your eligibility.

04

Performance Points System

Students earn performance points throughout the course based on test scores, attendance, class participation and assignments.

05

Internship Eligibility

Only students who meet the attendance threshold, clear all tests and earn the required performance points are eligible for internship.

06

Final Certificate

The TIMS certificate is awarded upon successful completion of the course, internship (where applicable), and all assessments.

Earn Your TIMS Certificate

Upon meeting all requirements — attendance, tests, performance points and internship — you receive the official TIMS Institute Certificate, a credential that demonstrates practical, real-world competency to employers and clients.
