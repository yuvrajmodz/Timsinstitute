Programme Rules

How The Internship Works

The TIMS internship is a merit-based, structured programme. Only students who meet all requirements are eligible. Here's everything you need to know.

01

Mandatory Internship

An internship component is a compulsory part of the eligible student's programme completion. It is not optional — you must participate to earn the final certificate.

02

85% Attendance Required

A minimum of 85% attendance throughout the entire course is required. Students who fall below this threshold will not be eligible for the internship or the final certificate.

03

Compulsory Tests

All module-end and in-class tests are compulsory. Absence from a test without valid, prior-approved reason may affect your eligibility.

04

Performance Points System

Points are earned throughout the course based on test scores, attendance, class participation and assignment completion. These points determine internship eligibility.

05

Internship Eligibility

Only students who have met the attendance threshold, cleared all compulsory tests, and accumulated the required performance points will be considered eligible for the internship placement.

06

Final Certificate

The official TIMS Institute Certificate is awarded to students who complete the course, fulfil the internship (where applicable) and pass all required assessments.

Your Journey

From Enrolment to Certificate

A clear path from joining TIMS to earning your professional certificate.

Step 01

Enrol & Attend Free Demo

Experience 2 days of live classes completely free. Once you decide to join, complete your enrolment and course fee payment.

Step 02

Complete Your Coursework

Attend classes, participate actively, complete all assignments. Maintain minimum 85% attendance throughout the programme.

Step 03

Clear All Compulsory Tests

Appear for all module-end tests. Your performance in these tests contributes to your overall performance points score.

Step 04

Earn Performance Points

Points are tallied across attendance, test scores, class participation and assignments. Eligible students proceed to the internship stage.

Step 05

Complete the Internship

Eligible students participate in the structured internship programme — gaining hands-on professional experience in a real-world setting.

Final Step

Receive Your Certificate

Upon successful completion of all requirements, you receive the official TIMS Institute Certificate — a mark of practical, professional achievement.

TIMS Institute Certificate

The TIMS Certificate is issued to students who meet all requirements — attendance, tests, performance points and internship. It reflects genuine competency and real-world readiness, not just course completion.
